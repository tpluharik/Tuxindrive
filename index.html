Your cloud drives.
Your mail attachments.
One place to search.

TuxInDrive connects your configured cloud accounts, synchronizes folders and searches files across your online drives. Its mail feature adds Gmail and Microsoft 365 mailboxes through browser authorization and builds a local attachment index on your Linux desktop.

DownloadsMail feature guide

Find attachments

Search attachment names, message subjects and senders, then open the original email in Gmail or Outlook. Mail search uses the last completed local index, not a new mailbox query for every keystroke.

Read-only access

Each mailbox requires its own consent. TuxInDrive does not send, edit or delete Gmail messages. Gmail authorization and Google Drive authorization are separate.

Local processing

Mail indexing runs on your computer. OAuth tokens stay in your native credential store. Supported attachment-text indexing is optional and off by default.

Gmail sign-in availability

Gmail currently requires a configured Desktop OAuth application. The shared TuxInDrive Mail application is being prepared for Google's public verification. Universal one-click Gmail sign-in is not yet approved or available to all users.

This is a reusable product feature for independently consenting users, not an integration limited to one email address.

Your mail data and choices

Attachment metadata and optional extracted text are stored in a private local search cache. That cache is not encrypted by the app; protect your device and backups. You can disconnect a mailbox and remove its local index without changing remote mail.

Read the TuxInDrive Mail privacy policy for access, storage, sharing and removal details.
